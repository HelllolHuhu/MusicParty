 ┃   ### 🎛️ Master Prompt: Beginner-Friendly FL Studio Web DAW                                                                                                                                                  
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                              
 ┃     Create a modern, intuitive in-browser Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) inspired by FL Studio, designed to be dead-simple for beginners yet powerful enough to produce complete beats (drums, basslines,   
 ┃   melodies, and vocals).                                                                                                                                                                                     
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                              
 ┃     ### 1. Core Architecture (The 3 FL Studio Pillars)                                                                                                                                                       
 ┃     1. **Channel Rack / Step Sequencer (Beat Maker)**:                                                                                                                                                       
 ┃        - 16-step / 32-step grid per channel with 4-beat color grouping (steps 1–4, 5–8, 9–12, 13–16).                                                                                                        
 ┃        - Instant click-to-toggle drum pads (Kick, Snare/Clap, Closed Hi-Hat, Open Hi-Hat, 808 Bass, Perc).                                                                                                   
 ┃        - Channel controls: Volume slider, Pan, Mute, Solo, and Sound Selector dropdown.                                                                                                                      
 ┃        - Presets dropdown with instant genre templates (Trap bounce, House 4-on-the-floor, Boom-Bap, Lo-Fi chill, Reggaeton dembow).                                                                         
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                              
 ┃     2. **Piano Roll (Melody & Bassline Editor)**:                                                                                                                                                            
 ┃        - Visual 2-octave grid (C3 to B4) with scale lock (Major, Minor, Pentatonic, Trap/Phrygian) to prevent players from hitting "wrong notes".                                                            
 ┃        - Click to place notes, drag right edge to adjust note length, right-click/click-again to delete.                                                                                                     
 ┃        - Multiple instrument presets: 808 Sub Bass, Pluck Synth, Electric Piano (Keys), Lead Synth, Retro Pad.                                                                                               
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                              
 ┃     3. **Playlist / Arrangement Timeline**:                                                                                                                                                                  
 ┃        - Multi-track timeline (Tracks 1 to 6) with snap-to-grid (1 bar / 1/2 bar / 1/4 bar).                                                                                                                 
 ┃        - Paint mode / Stamp mode: Click pattern blocks (Pattern 1 Drums, Pattern 2 Bass, Pattern 3 Melody, Recorded Vocals) and stamp them onto the playlist tracks.                                         
 ┃        - Interactive top ruler bar: Click or drag scrubber to jump playhead anywhere, with loop region markers.                                                                                              
 ┃        - Audio lane for Microphone Vocal Takes: Click record, sing/rap with countdown, and place the resulting waveform directly on the timeline with exact recorded duration.                               
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                              
 ┃     ### 2. Sound Library & Audio Engine (Tone.js)                                                                                                                                                            
 ┃     - **High-Quality Sound Engine**:                                                                                                                                                                         
 ┃       - `Tone.MembraneSynth` & `Tone.Sampler` for punchy kicks and 808s.                                                                                                                                     
 ┃       - `Tone.NoiseSynth` & `Tone.MetalSynth` for crisp hats, claps, and open cymbals.                                                                                                                       
 ┃       - `Tone.PolySynth` with filter envelope for rich chords and lush melodies.                                                                                                                             
 ┃     - **Global Transport Controls**:                                                                                                                                                                         
 ┃       - Play / Pause (Spacebar shortcut), Stop / Return to start, Record button.                                                                                                                             
 ┃       - BPM slider (60 to 180 BPM, default 130 BPM) with Tap Tempo.                                                                                                                                          
 ┃       - Swing / Groove percentage slider for authentic bounce.                                                                                                                                               
 ┃       - Master volume meter with live visual equalizer bars.                                                                                                                                                 
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                              
 ┃     ### 3. UX & Beginner-Friendly UI Design                                                                                                                                                                  
 ┃     - **Single-Screen Neobrutalist / Modern Dark Layout**:                                                                                                                                                   
 ┃       - **Top Toolbar**: Transport (Play/Pause/BPM/Timer), Mode switcher tabs (`Step Sequencer`, `Piano Roll`, `Arranger / Playlist`), and Export/Finish button.                                             
 ┃       - **Left Sidebar**: Preset sounds, loops, and quick one-shot library with audio preview on hover/click.                                                                                                
 ┃       - **Center Workspace**: Active tab view (Sequencer grid, Piano roll, or Playlist lanes) with clear visual feedback, playhead needle, and animated step highlights.                                     
 ┃       - **Micro-Tutorial Hints**: Floating 1-line tooltips (e.g., *"Click pads to make a drum rhythm, then switch to Arranger to lay out your song"*).                                                       
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                              
 ┃     ### 4. Multiplayer & State Management                                                                                                                                                                    
 ┃     - Pure in-memory reactive state (React 19 / Vite / Tailwind CSS).                                                                                                                                        
 ┃     - Clean JSON export format containing BPM, tracks, step sequencer patterns, piano roll notes, and vocal URLs for effortless multiplayer synchronization, Firebase real-time sharing, and presentation playback.                                                                                                                                                                           
 ┃   ──────                                                                                                                                                                                           
 ┃   ### 💡 Why this design works so well for players:                                                                                                                                                
 ┃                                                                                                                                                                                                    
 ┃   1. Instant gratification (Step Sequencer): Players don't need music theory; clicking a few boxes on steps 1, 5, 9, 13 immediately creates a banging drum beat.                                   
 ┃   2. Scale Snap (Piano Roll): Locking the notes to Pentatonic or Minor scales ensures whatever notes they click sound harmonious and musical.                                                      
 ┃   3. Pattern-based Arranging (Playlist): Just like real FL Studio, turning beats into patterns and stamping them across a 30s timeline allows complex multi-section arrangements without timeline  
 ┃   clutter.
